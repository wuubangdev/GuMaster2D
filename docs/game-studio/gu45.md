# Gu illustration and casting art — 0.45

Scope is the four playable catalog entries GU-01, GU-03, GU-05 and GU-07. The catalog contains four active Gu; this pass does not invent three missing passive entries. It changes presentation only. Existing balance, inventory IDs, upgrade/fusion transactions and save compatibility are retained.

## Visual identity

- Nguyệt Quang: a living jade moth with paired crescent wings; hooked wing-shaped projectile and thin silk wake.
- Bạch Ngọc: six-legged jade scarab; separate elytra plates form the guard, leaving the character's body visible.
- Bạch Hạc: spirit insect with long ivory feather wings; short feather and wind traces distinguish dash from offensive spells.
- Băng Tằm: curled silkworm with crystal horn and silk filament; frozen cocoon spindle and crystal contact fragments.

Four original SVG sources in `assets/gu/illustrated` are authored by `tools/author_gu45_art.py`. Their transparent 256px imports serve the 32/48px HUD and larger native cultivation portrait. `GuSkillArt` caches imported textures; `GuPixelUI.icon` preserves the unavailable-state dimming contract. No random line noise or animated texture grain is introduced.

## Action contracts

`WorldEffects.cast_stage` names windup, flight, contact and finished stages. Sword release remains `GuCharacterActionMotion.release_seconds("sword")` (0.2176s), other projectiles 0.08s, and contact remains 0.28s. Before release, the current right hand supplies the origin. Release freezes the actual hand offset; contact freezes the target point. Moon flight has a direct path; ice flight lifts at most 9 native pixels while preserving exact endpoints. Accepted event dictionaries are copied, never mutated. No VFX applies damage or grants rewards.

The actor owns a single shield silhouette: opening during its 0.48s defend action, then the steady guard loop. WorldEffects only adds activation runes while an actor is bound, avoiding double shell outlines.

Reduced motion holds a compact creature motif at the hand until contact; it no longer paints an early hit on the target. Contact then fades as one stable silhouette. Harvest cancellation by action serial is unchanged.

Enemy impacts share `BeastAttackGeometry` polygons with the authority and windup warnings. Melee/cone terminate inside their locked sectors; charge uses its accepted capsule; spit/stomp/burst use their exact one/three-circle centers. Impact boundaries stay fixed and fade quickly. Decorative droplets/chips indicate material and direction; they do not imply an expanding damage radius.
